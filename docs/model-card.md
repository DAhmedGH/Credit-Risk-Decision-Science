# Model card

**Model:** xgboost_05_engineered_none, 100 trees, unweighted, financial-row-v1.1 combined 32 financial predictors. Selected by development CV AP/practical tie/log loss/simplicity; raw mapping selected by policy-validation log loss. Phase 4 run 9ca62eca2665438c9c97b4889413acfa, bundle SHA 45d24e21b67e27c5a522863457d7774536b6af3608bb5e9bf6e6b06c030bdbcd. No refitting after freeze.

**Task/population:** Released next-month default indicator, class 1, for existing Taiwanese credit-card accounts at the released month-end snapshot. April–September 2005 history is assumed available, including all September fields. One row is one customer snapshot. Intended use is education and benchmark research; hypothetical policy simulation has no validated loan-origination interpretation.

**Source:** 30,000 supplied-workbook records compatible with UCI 350, Yeh (2009), DOI 10.24432/C55S3H, CC BY 4.0. Workbook hash 30c6be3abd8dcfd3e6096c828bad8c2f011238620f5369220bd60cfc82700933. Original retrieval time/download chain and official ZIP bytes are unknown. Source/curated/exported counts 30,000; exclusions 0. Preserve signed bills and special repayment codes.

**Features:** 19 raw financial predictors plus 13 deterministic row proxies. No IDs, target, sex, age, education or marital status in the primary contract. Payment/balance ratios are proxies, not contractual repayment or debt-to-income. Frozen development-fitted medians/vocabularies and preprocessing are packaged with the model. Unknown statuses use frozen all-zero one-hot handling; row overflow/nonfinite/nonnumeric/invalid payment or limit is quarantined.

**Validation:** Frozen development/calibration/policy-validation/test counts 18,000/4,500/3,000/4,500; seed 42; identical financial fingerprints kept together across partitions and five development folds. Test has 4,378 clusters. Calibration uses 4,500 rows excluded from base fitting; policy selection uses 3,000 different rows. Raw/sigmoid/isotonic validation log loss 0.43299/0.43623/0.45599. No test evidence changes selection.

**Frozen final evaluation:** AP 0.56422 [0.53375,0.59580]; ROC-AUC 0.79398 [0.77763,0.80965]; log loss 0.42331 [0.40652,0.44119]; Brier 0.13320 [0.12662,0.14062]. 1,000 seed 42 paired fingerprint-group draws and 95% percentile intervals describe sample uncertainty conditional on fixed models/policies. They omit model-development uncertainty and temporal/external robustness. Forest AP 0.56636 does not replace the frozen champion. [Full comparisons](../reports/results.md).

**Policy:** Strict p<cutoff, ties abstain. Base payoffs G 50/L 500/C 0 are dimensionless synthetic utility units. Frozen empirical cutoff 0.085 accepts 1,028/4,500 accounts (22.844%), 55 defaults (5.350% accepted default rate), and yields 21,150 benchmark units, 4.70/customer [2.9012,6.3711]. Analytic cutoff 50/550 yields 5.0667/customer; empirical-minus-analytic interval includes zero. All 88 policy/scenario rows are published, including abstain-all at G 25/L 750 and accept-none default rate N/A. No profit, avoided-loss or causal claim.

**Explainability/audit:** SHAP explains the selected raw XGBoost's default log odds with fixed development background and preselected policy-validation cases, not causation or intervention effects. Demographic summaries are descriptive policy-validation audits, suppressed at n<100 or fewer than 20 of either class. No group-specific optimization or fairness certification; excluded demographics may still have financial proxies.

**Delivery/monitoring:** Unlabeled CSV/Parquet scoring preserves IDs, rejects target/extra/fatal schemas, quarantines row errors and reconciles counts. No fit or database access. Synthetic unchanged-record mix shifts retain meaningful benchmark labels; corruption removes labels. Development PSI bins include tails/missingness and epsilon 1e-6; warnings are heuristics, not observed temporal or concept drift. No automatic retraining.

**Limitations:** Historical Taiwan existing accounts; no new-applicant or Canadian representativeness, lifetime-default prediction, temporal/external cohort validation, observed intervention evidence or production deployment. A full-data refit is a new bundle requiring new evidence. Trusted local serialized files only.
